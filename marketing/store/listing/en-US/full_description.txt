Manager runs your whole store from one screen, on a phone or a tablet: install the same app and it adapts to the screen size. It works offline first. Sell through any outage: offline work syncs back by itself, and anything that snags waits for you to retry or discard it. Sell from the till: point the camera at any barcode and every scan lands in the cart, with totals that update as you go. Take cash, or a pay-link QR the customer scans on their own phone. No network? The sale still goes through offline: a six-digit code confirms it, and it syncs itself when you are back online. Check the receipt on screen before it prints. Manager is both your POS and your back office, so you do not need a second app to take orders: ring up walk-in sales at the till, and take pickup, delivery and dine-in orders in the same app. Run the shop: accept incoming orders automatically, move them through accepted, cooking, ready and on the way, and see every order in one queue. Take walk-in, pickup, delivery and dine-in orders, and send them to the kitchen display, where every dish has a live clock. Keep your menu in order: products, add-ons and extras, priced and stocked, with quick stock adjustments saved in one pass. Manage your delivery stock on the road: issue consignment loads to your drivers, follow what each load sells, and settle what comes back when it closes. See where the money is: today, this week or this month, with every rand earned charted day by day, and the profit each product really makes. Pick your language once in the language sheet and every screen follows. Manager is the store app of a white-label delivery platform, for the shops that sell on it.
