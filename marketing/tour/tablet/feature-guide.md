# Manager — Feature Guide

Run your store from your pocket

> This guide is generated automatically on every merge: CI builds the
> app, walks the guided tour on an Android emulator, and captures
> each screen below.

## 1. Welcome to Manager

![Welcome to Manager](screenshots/01-welcome.png)

Manager runs your whole store from one screen - sign in to get started.

## 2. Sign in

![Sign in](screenshots/02-auth_login.png)

Signing in to Manager is quick and simple.

## 3. Create an account

![Create an account](screenshots/03-auth_register.png)

New to Manager? Create your account in a few easy steps.

## 4. Reset your password

![Reset your password](screenshots/04-auth_reset_password.png)

Forgot your password? Manager gets you back in with a secure reset.

## 5. Sell straight from the camera

![Sell straight from the camera](screenshots/05-pos_scan.png)

The till is the first thing a store owner sees - point it at any barcode and
every scan lands the product in the cart, with torch and battery-saving auto-
pause built in.

## 6. The cart keeps up

![The cart keeps up](screenshots/06-pos_cart.png)

Steppers, decimal weights for loose goods, live formatted totals - and Clear All
for when the customer changes their mind.

## 7. Cash, QR - or offline by code

![Cash, QR - or offline by code](screenshots/07-pos_checkout.png)

Take cash or a pay-link QR the customer scans on their own phone; offline, a
6-digit code confirms it and the sale syncs itself later.

## 8. See the receipt before it prints

![See the receipt before it prints](screenshots/08-pos_receipt_preview.png)

Print Receipt & Finish lands on the paper first - the slip exactly as it will
print, line by line - so a receipt never fires blind, and one tap finishes the
sale from there.

## 9. Everything else, one tap away

![Everything else, one tap away](screenshots/09-restaurant_hub.png)

The restaurant hub is the spine of the app - your shop's identity up top, and
every setting, section and report a tap below it.

## 10. Nothing gets lost offline

![Nothing gets lost offline](screenshots/10-sync_issues.png)

Sell through any outage - offline work syncs back by itself, and anything that
snags waits here with retry or discard.

## 11. Your menu, organised

![Your menu, organised](screenshots/11-menu.png)

Your whole menu in one place - products, add-ons and extras, each on its own
tab, priced and stocked.

## 12. List a product in minutes

![List a product in minutes](screenshots/12-add_product.png)

Name it, price it, stock it - a new product is ready to sell in minutes.

## 13. Every order, one queue

![Every order, one queue](screenshots/13-order_queue.png)

New, accepted, ready, on the way - every order moves through one queue the
moment it lands. On the launcher, the manager window counts what is waiting on
you and keeps the takings inside the app.

## 14. The paper trail, kept

![The paper trail, kept](screenshots/14-order_history.png)

Every completed sale stays on record - scroll back through your full history any
time.

## 15. The kitchen sees every order

![The kitchen sees every order](screenshots/15-kitchen_queue.png)

Accepted, cooking, ready - the kitchen queue tracks every dish with a live
clock, so nothing burns and nothing waits.

## 16. Speak your language

![Speak your language](screenshots/16-comms_language.png)

Manager speaks your language - pick it once in the language sheet and every
screen follows.

## 17. Money at a glance

![Money at a glance](screenshots/17-revenue_income.png)

Today, this week or this month - every rand earned, charted day by day.

## 18. Plans without surprises

![Plans without surprises](screenshots/18-subscriptions_plans.png)

Compare plans side by side and switch in a tap - clear pricing, no surprises.

## 19. Plan your day in seconds

![Plan your day in seconds](screenshots/19-productivity_tasks.png)

Capture to-dos with subtasks and categories - your day, planned before it
starts.

## 20. A service run from a template

![A service run from a template](screenshots/20-productivity_task_compose.png)

Softener, megaChar, filters and membranes - one tap fills the task with every
step and its timing.

## 21. Readings before it goes back to service

![Readings before it goes back to service](screenshots/21-productivity_maintenance_readings.png)

TDS and pressure, checked against the plant's limits - out of spec is named and
holds the finish until it is re-tested or explained.

## 22. A photo and a note, if you want them

![A photo and a note, if you want them](screenshots/22-productivity_maintenance_photo.png)

The last step is optional - Skip is live beside Finish, and nothing here can
trap you mid-run.

## 23. A calculator, right where you work

![A calculator, right where you work](screenshots/23-calc_keypad.png)

Quick sums without switching apps - a full calculator built in.
